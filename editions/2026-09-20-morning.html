Anthropic says Claude now leads 26% of the work building its next AI models

A company that builds AI now says its AI is doing a quarter of the building.

Anthropic told the Associated Press that Claude leads roughly 26 percent of the research and development behind its next models, up from no measurable contribution in February. About 90 percent of that research and development now happens in collaboration with the model, which the company describes as doing large chunks of work under close human direction rather than working on its own. Anthropic said roughly 30,000 agents were carrying out research and engineering tasks in August. The company framed the disclosure around a principle it stated plainly: it should do everything possible to minimize the gap between what frontier labs know and what the public knows.

You had many sons and much sorrow among them. What would you tell a parent whose grown child has walked away?

So hear me plainly: a child who walks away is still walking, and the road is longer than your worst night. Do not mistake silence for the end of the story. Keep the lamp lit and the door unbarred. Do not chase him down with arguments; he has heard them all already, and in your own voice. Pray for him by name more often than you speak of him to others. And be gentle with yourself about the years you would rebuild if you could, for I learned late that a man may be skilled with a harp and clumsy in his own house. Then wait. Waiting is not nothing. It is the hardest work love ever does.
