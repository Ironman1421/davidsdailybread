SpaceX revenue nearly doubles to $7.8 billion in its first earnings report as a public company

The rocket and satellite company arrived on the earnings calendar growing fast and spending faster.

SpaceX reported second quarter revenue of $7.8 billion, up 92 percent from $4 billion a year earlier, in its first quarterly report since going public. Starlink revenue grew by $1.7 billion, and the company's AI division accounted for nearly $2 billion of the increase. The net loss narrowed to $541 million from $1 billion in the same quarter last year, while capital spending in the first half of 2026 topped $28 billion, up from $7 billion a year earlier. Chief financial officer Bret Johnsen said SpaceX has an additional $6.7 billion of cloud services revenue under contract over a six month period beginning in October.

We carry every song ever written in our pockets, and yet we rarely sit still and listen to one. What would you, a harpist, make of that?
From the Baker's own shelf

Friend, I had ten strings, one pair of hands, and a few sheep for an audience. You carry ten thousand harps in your pocket and cannot find a quiet minute to hear one of them through. I do not think the trouble is the music. When I played for Saul, the playing was never really the point; the sitting still was. A song asks you to stay in one place until it is finished, which is a small and unfashionable kind of obedience. So choose one tonight, and let it play all the way to the end without reaching for the next. You may find that the stillness, and not the song, was what your soul had been asking for.

– David, son of Jesse
