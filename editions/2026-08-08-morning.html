Business & markets

US employers cut 23,000 jobs in July, and revisions erased another 103,000 from May and June

The July report did not simply miss expectations; it rewrote the two months in front of it.

Nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000 in July, against forecasts that had ranged from 83,000 to 95,000 new jobs. Revisions cut May's gain by 66,000 to 63,000 and June's by 37,000 to 20,000, a combined reduction of 103,000. The unemployment rate slipped to 4.1 percent from 4.2 percent, but the labor force participation rate fell to 61.4 percent, its lowest level in more than five years. Local government education shed 50,000 jobs and leisure and hospitality lost 40,000, while health care added 22,000, below its 12-month average of 36,000.

01

Researchers generated quantum entanglement from sunlight rather than a laser

A Fresnel lens, not a laser A University of Ottawa and Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light team concentrated sunlight through a window-sized lens into a hair-width fiber and a millimeter-sized crystal, producing entangled photons at about 94 percent fidelity that violated Bell's inequality.

Letters to the King
Some of your psalms argue with God and accuse Him of sleeping. Is a person allowed to be angry with Him, and does saying it out loud change anything?
From the Baker's own shelf

Ah, you have been reading with your eyes open, and I will not pretend otherwise. I am the man who told the Almighty to wake up and rise to my defense, as though He had nodded off at His post, and who asked how long He intended to forget me, forever perhaps. The sleeping line you remember belongs to the sons of Korah, and they were bolder than I was. Here is what the caves taught me: God is not fragile, and He has never once asked me to pretend. Those quarreling songs are not slips the scribes forgot to cut. They were kept on purpose, so that you would know the door stays open even when you arrive at it angry. Anger spoken to Him is prayer. Anger swallowed only curdles, and soon enough a man stops coming at all. Does saying it aloud change anything? It changed me. Watch how the complaints end: I begin by accusing and I finish by remembering, not because a friend told me to cheer up, but because a grievance carried the whole way to His face runs out of road and finds mercy standing there. So bring Him the honest sentence, dear one. Only mind this, from an old man who wrote a great many of them: say it to Him, not merely about Him. All the difference in the world lives in that.

– David, son of Jesse
