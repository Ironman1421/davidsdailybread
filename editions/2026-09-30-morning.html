OpenAI ships GPT-6.1 Sol at one-fifth the token price of GPT-6 Astra, with more than 20 DevDay announcements

Cheaper frontier coding, hosted agents, and a shared team workspace all landed on the same day.

OpenAI used DevDay on Tuesday to introduce GPT-6.1 Sol, a model it says improves coding and computer use while charging one-fifth of GPT-6 Astra's standard token prices. The company also opened a public beta of an Agents API with hosted execution, memory, tool integration, and multi-agent coordination, moved Codex to a cloud mode that runs tasks in the background, and added a paid Ultrafast tier it says generates tokens up to eight times faster in Codex and six times faster in the API. Always-on agents called Dots began rolling out to Pro subscribers on web, mobile, and desktop, excluding the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. A new ChatGPT Space gives teams a shared home for files and project context, and a Pro 500 plan raises the usage allowance twenty-five times.

Letters to the King
How do you lead people who remember exactly what you used to be?
From the Baker's own shelf

Dear friend, they remember the boy with the sling, the harp, and the smell of sheep that no crown ever quite scrubs out. Let them. I learned early that a man who spends his strength hiding his smaller self has none left over for leading, so I stopped arguing with the memory and let the work answer it instead. Lead the way you would have wanted to be led back when you were the small one they recall: plainly, patiently, and without pretending you arrived by some grander road than the one they watched you walk. They saw you stumble, which means they will also see you rise, and the rising will teach them more than any speech you could polish. And on the day pride whispers that you have outgrown the people who knew you first, go stand a moment in the pasture of your memory. The God who called me out of it was never once embarrassed by the sheep.

– David, son of Jesse
