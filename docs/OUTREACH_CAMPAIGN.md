requires the operator to hold rights to uploaded images and to review generated
video before relying on or publishing it. See Google's
[Gemini Omni help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339),
[Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms),
[Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961),
and [Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy).
This dated review is an operating control, not legal advice.

generates, but the operator remains responsible for having rights to inputs and
for avoiding infringement, deception, and privacy harm. Consumer Gemini chats
can be retained and, depending on settings, used to improve services or
reviewed by people. DDB therefore applies the stricter boundary below.

## IP and confidentiality strategy

The campaign publishes finished value, not the repeatable production recipe.
Public material may include approved editions, excerpts, source links,
corrections, and finished derivatives. Internal calendars, scripts, approval
cards, watchlists, and aggregate performance analysis stay internal. Master
prompts, source weighting, automation, account recovery data, credentials,
private analytics, unpublished roadmaps, and reader or spiritual data stay
restricted.

The format can be copied. The defense is the dated archive, consistent daily
ritual, source and correction receipts, trusted relationships, operating
discipline, and faster learning. Preserve evidence of name and content use, use
the name consistently, do not claim registration, and trigger focused name
clearance when traction, spending, partnership, merchandise, app-store, or a
confusing third-party use makes the risk material.

## Approval, stop, and rollback

David separately approves every original post, reply, provider input package,
and final Omni publication package. Silence is rejection. Approval cannot be
reused after the parent, text, source, asset, account, output, or context
changes.

Stop immediately on an account warning, security concern, hidden or probable-
spam reply, unsupported claim, material correction, unknown right, private-data
exposure, provider-policy uncertainty, unapproved cost, weakened edition,
duplicate publication, or wrong-account action.

Rollback means stop new campaign preparation and publication, preserve the
incident receipt, remove or correct the affected derivative through the
official provider, and change the smallest declared variable only after review.
It never rewrites the canonical archive to hide an error.
