# Founder-led audience validation roadmap

Status: active validation strategy; reforecast from measured behavior

Owner and final decision-maker: David Friedhof

Last reconciled: 2026-07-31

## Current objective

Prove that the first 1,000 genuinely engaged people will return to David's Daily
Bread for faith, technology, prayer, and service while David retains the
mission, theology, editorial direction, partnerships, product direction,
channels, and monetization. This is a quality and culture milestone, not a
deadline or permission to chase follower count at the expense of trust.

The former one-million-followers-in-six-months target is no longer the active
operating goal. Larger reach remains possible after a trustworthy, repeatable
foundation exists.

## Positioning

David's Daily Bread is a founder-led Christian media and learning project:

> Grow in faith. Understand technology wisely. Pray for one another. Use what
> you learn in service to others.

The website, archive, and RSS are the permanent home. X and other individually
approved social channels create discovery and discussion, then point people
back to the durable work. David may remain off camera; visual consistency and a
clear editorial voice can carry the identity.

## The smallest useful community

Community means repeated public practices, not a new platform or shared
governance. Initial experiments may include:

- source-linked website editions and practical faith-and-technology guides;
- useful X posts and individually approved, manually posted replies;
- a public prayer discussion only on an already-approved channel, with David's
  approval of the exact post, rules, moderation owner, and time window; and
- carefully reviewed contributions from the frozen existing editorial queue.

New reader submissions are paused until a private intake boundary is verified
and David explicitly approves reopening. Do not invite submissions or direct
growth traffic to an old form while the pause is in force.

Public prayer discussions require active moderation. Do not permit identifying
details about other people, addresses or phone numbers, requests for money,
accusations, political campaigning disguised as prayer, medical claims that
replace professional care, romantic solicitation, spam, or private recruitment.
David retains removal and final editorial authority.

Do not build or provision custom community software, member accounts, a social
feed, open posting, or private/direct messaging under this roadmap.

## Three validation phases

### Phase 1: clarify and listen

- Make the founder doctrine and faith-and-technology promise visible in
  planning and channel decisions.
- Record the actual baseline for website return, RSS, X, and any approved social
  channel. Unknown values remain unknown, never zero.
- Test a small number of repeatable formats derived from canonical website work.
- Listen through already-approved public channels without transferring
  editorial, theological, or governance authority.

Exit: people can accurately describe the promise, and the project can evaluate
returning attention without collecting unnecessary personal data.

### Phase 2: establish rhythm

- Keep only formats that earn repeat reading, saves, shares, thoughtful replies,
  or useful discussion.
- Develop a recognizable cadence for technology learning, Christian reflection,
  and carefully moderated public prayer where specifically approved.
- Invite suggestions without transferring editorial or theological control.
- Document moderation load, recurring participants, and examples of service
  enabled by the work.

Exit: a stable group returns voluntarily, the moderation burden is responsible,
and at least two formats provide repeatable value.

### Phase 3: reach the first 1,000 engaged returning people

- Expand only the formats and channels that preserve quality and founder
  direction.
- Reuse canonical work natively across approved social channels instead of
  building channel-specific institutions.
- Collaborate selectively only after mission, rights, disclosure, final
  editorial authority, and the exact partnership are approved by David.
- Review what participants actually ask for before proposing any new product or
  organizational structure.

Exit: 1,000 people have demonstrated meaningful, repeat engagement through a
documented mix of returning readership, RSS follows, constructive public
participation, and approved social interaction. Measurement must be
privacy-safe, avoid double-counting across channels, and be explicitly approved
before production collection begins.

## Measures and guardrails

Review weekly where privacy-safe evidence already exists:

- returning readers and engaged time on the website;
- RSS follows and repeat opens;
- social shares, saves, thoughtful replies, profile visits, follows, and site
  visits;
- repeat participants in approved moderated public discussions;
- moderation incidents, response load, corrections, and unsupported claims; and
- production time, channel reliability, and any explicitly approved cost.

Follower count is reach evidence, not the definition of community. Never buy
followers, use engagement pods, fabricate urgency, lower sourcing standards, or
trade privacy and dignity for growth.

No spend, new channel, partnership, publishing automation, service, account,
resource, credential, provisioning, or deployment is authorized by this
roadmap. Each requires David's explicit approval and its normal repository
guardrails.

## Delayed decisions

The following are not current roadmap work: newsletter activation or sending,
new reader intake, nonprofit formation, a governing board, custom community
software, member accounts, open posting, and private/direct messaging.
Cloudflare Workers + D1 audience measurement and the Supabase reader store may
be designed and verified locally only; neither may be provisioned, linked,
deployed, canaried, or activated. Each delayed initiative requires a separate
explicit decision from David after demand, mission fit, moderation capacity,
privacy risk, operating ownership, and cost are understood.
