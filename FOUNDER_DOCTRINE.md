# David's Daily Bread founder operating doctrine

Status: active governing direction

Founder and final decision-maker: David Friedhof

Effective: 2026-07-31

## Mission

David's Daily Bread is a founder-led Christian media and learning project. It
exists to help believers grow in faith, understand technology wisely, pray for
one another, and use what they learn in service to others.

The project will pursue truth without hype, conviction without cruelty,
technology without worshiping it, and community without exploiting
vulnerability.

## Current operating model

- The website, archive, and RSS are the permanent canonical home.
- X and other individually approved social channels are for discovery,
  discussion, and audience growth. They point people back to durable work on
  the website and do not replace it.
- The project remains founder-led and founder-directed. Listening, comments,
  suggestions, prayer, and future contributions do not transfer governance.
- David retains final control over the mission, theology, editorial direction,
  partnerships, product direction, channels, and monetization.
- The current proof goal is the first 1,000 genuinely engaged people who return
  for faith, technology, prayer, and service, not organizational scale for its
  own sake.

## Current boundaries

Until David explicitly records a different decision in the repository:

- Do not send a newsletter or perform newsletter activation work. Do not draft
  an issue, schedule or test a send, configure a sending address, import a list,
  add sending credentials, or advance activation gates. The existing live
  signup page may remain unchanged, but its presence is not approval to send.
- Do not form a nonprofit, recruit a governing board, or give outside parties
  authority over the project.
- Do not build or provision custom community software, member accounts, a
  social feed, open member posting, or private/direct messaging.
- Any prayer interaction must occur as a public, carefully moderated discussion
  on an approved existing channel. It must protect privacy, prohibit requests
  for money and identifying details, and remain subject to David's moderation
  and removal decisions.
- New Ask the Baker, Letters to the King, and Crumb Board submissions are
  temporarily paused until a private intake boundary is verified and David
  explicitly approves reopening. The approved interim site change may be
  prepared locally, but the 2026-07-31 approval does not authorize deployment,
  external form changes, deletion, or history rewriting. Existing reviewed
  reader material remains an editorial queue, not an open community feed or
  private messaging surface.
- Cloudflare Workers + D1 is selected only for provider-specific local audience
  measurement implementation and an unprovisioned canary plan. No Cloudflare
  account, resource, credential, endpoint, canary execution, deployment,
  activation, production collection, baseline start, or spend is authorized by
  that selection.

## How new work is approved

Contributors and AI agents may research, draft, prototype locally, and recommend
changes within an assigned task. They may not infer permission to publish,
send, spend money, provision services, enter partnerships, change theology, or
expand governance. Silence, an old plan, a live signup form, or unfinished
infrastructure is not approval.

Delayed ideas may be reconsidered only after real demand is demonstrated through
repeat participation, returning readers, responsible moderation capacity, and a
clear mission fit. Newsletter sending, nonprofit formation, custom community
software, private messaging, open posting, and formal governance each require a
new explicit decision from David before planning or activation resumes.

## Authority and continuity

This doctrine governs mission, ownership, strategic direction, and the paused
initiatives above. `BRAND.md` remains authoritative for reader-visible brand and
house style; `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`, `docs/DISTRIBUTION_SPEC.md`, roadmaps,
runbooks, contracts, and tests implement the doctrine and must not contradict
it. `BAKE.md` remains the complete procedure for an already-approved daily bake.

When documents conflict within this scope, this doctrine wins. A future change
requires David's explicit decision and a repository update that reconciles the
affected specifications, machine-readable guards, and tests.
